Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2027
At Wassers & Co., safeguarding your privacy is as important to us as the purity of our water. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application (app.wassersand.co) and services in Surat, Gujarat.

1. Information We Collect
• Personal Data: Name, delivery address, phone number, and email address required to process and deliver your hydration orders.
• Location Data: We access device GPS strictly to provide accurate Live Rider Tracking via Google Maps API and to ensure precise doorstep delivery.
• Order History: To facilitate our "Pure Rewards" loyalty program and quick re-ordering.

2. Payment Security
All digital payments, including UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Wallets, are processed securely through our verified partner, Razorpay. Wassers & Co. does not store, process, or have access to your bank details or credit card numbers.

3. Data Storage & Protection
Your user profiles, wallet balances, and order histories are securely encrypted and stored on Google Firebase. We employ strict access controls to ensure your data is never sold or rented to third-party marketers.

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to delete your account, please contact our administrative office at wassersandco@gmail.com or call +91 846-9277377.
