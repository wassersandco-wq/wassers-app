Delivery & Return Policy

1. Delivery Coverage & Timings
Wassers & Co. currently operates its delivery fleet exclusively within the municipal limits of Surat, Gujarat. Orders placed via the app are dispatched through our live-tracked Maruti Suzuki Eeco fleet. Subscription deliveries (weekly/monthly) are fulfilled according to your pre-selected time slots.

2. 20L Water Capsule Exchange Protocol
When ordering a 20L Water Capsule Refill (₹80), the customer must provide an empty, undamaged 20-liter jar in exchange at the time of delivery. If an empty jar is not provided, a one-time refundable deposit will be charged per capsule.

3. Return & Refund Policy
Because packaged drinking water is a consumable product governed by strict FSSAI hygiene standards, we do not accept returns on any bottles or capsules once the factory seal has been broken.

4. Damaged Goods
If you receive a leaking bottle, a damaged 20L capsule, or a broken seal upon delivery, please refuse the item immediately with the rider. We will dispatch a free replacement within 24 hours. For pre-paid orders that are refused due to damage, full refunds are processed via Razorpay and will reflect in your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

5. Cancellation
Orders can be canceled directly through the app free of charge until the rider has departed our Begampura facility. Once the order status changes to "Live on Map," cancellations may be subject to a nominal delivery offset fee.
