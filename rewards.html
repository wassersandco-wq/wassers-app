Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Pure Rewards?
Pure Rewards is the official loyalty program for Wassers & Co. Earn points on every jar delivered to your home or office in Surat.
2. Do I need to pay to become a member?
No, joining is completely free! Just create an account on our app to start earning.
3. How do I earn points?
You earn points for every ₹1 spent on 20L capsules, 1L bottles, 500ml bottles, and 200ml mini-sips.
4. How do I redeem my points?
During checkout, you can apply your points directly to your cart to offset the cost of your order.
